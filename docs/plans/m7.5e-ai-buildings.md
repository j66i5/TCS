# M7.5e AI uses buildings: build plan

**Progress:** step 1 defend ✅ 2026-11-14 (DECISIONS "AI uses buildings, step defend"; the order field is
`windowIndex`, not `window`); step 2 leave ✅ 2026-11-14 (DECISIONS "AI uses buildings, step leave"; cut off = enemies
on two sides, not off the windows' front; only seen enemies count as inside; well-located contacts only; three radio
keys); step 3 clear ✅ 2026-11-14 (DECISIONS "AI uses buildings, step clear": inside throws from the doorway then
a step aside, none up the stairs, aim at the far corner, posted grenades `postErrorM`; clear.mts and batch targets
not met, see there); step 4 to come.

Owner request: ROADMAP M7.5 ("M7.5e AI uses buildings"); owner answers 2026-11-14 (binding):
1. **Both sides** use buildings: defenders hold houses; AI attackers clear the houses on their way and hold the
   ones they take.
2. **Defending squad spread: a mix.** Part of the squad inside at windows (upper floors where useful), part outside
   covering the approach and the back, so that one grenade or shell cannot catch them all; a few men per room at
   most.
3. **Leaving.** Men leave a house when it becomes a trap (grenades or high explosive land in or on it), when the
   enemy gets inside, or when it is cut off from behind (outflanked).
4. **Clearing.** Suppress the windows, get close, throw a grenade through a window or door, then go in room by room.
Owner, same day: the harder village is accepted. The village attack is **not** re-tuned for balance alone; balance
is reported at every step.

Four steps, built in this order: **defend**, **leave**, **clear**, **polish**. Each step ends with `npm run check`
run alone, its batches, a DECISIONS.md entry, and (where the catalog fingerprint changes) `npm run golden:update`.
`packages/tools/golden/no-interiors/*` is never touched.

## 0. Facts the plan depends on (code at dbdb479)
- **The AI defender never goes into a house.** `ai/src/siting.ts` `siteDefence` drops every candidate with
  `indoorAt(...) >= 0`, and so do the commander's rally points and support-line places. The few defenders seen
  indoors in the baseline ran in under mortar fire (package B `leaveBeatenZone`: a man in the open within 30 m of a
  house goes in) or were pushed in by a move.
- **Men indoors never leave on their own.** `leaveBeatenZone` returns at once for a man indoors ("its walls and
  floors are his cover"); `stepAside` leaves indoor men alone; no rule looks at grenades or enemies inside his house.
- **The AI attacker has no house drill.** The assault is the generic `assault` drill (`drills.ts`: bounds, then a
  last rush that spreads the men around the objective point with `freeSpotNear`, outdoors). Grenades come from the
  commander's `grenade` orders at known enemies within 35 m (launchers from 200 m after closing) and from package D
  (`ownGrenade`: an enemy behind cover or in a house within throwing range).
- **What exists to build on.** `occupy` order + `occupyBuilding`/`occupyPlaces`/`windowPlaces` (`systems/occupy.ts`:
  window places on every floor, posture by sill, facing out); `indoorSpots` (about one man per 4.5 m²);
  `interiorGraph` (rooms, doorway nodes, stairs); `routeBetween` + `routeWaypoints` with heights; `standZ`;
  `floorOf`/`floorAtZ`; `throwArcExists` and the generic `grenade` action (rises him for the throw, keeps the grenade
  if no arc gets there); `FireTask.suppress` (fire into cover, covering-fire pick); `homePosture` (package C).
- `Drill = {kind: "assault"|"withdraw"|"rout", x, y, groups, mover, phaseTick, final, trailTick?, waitFire?, stepped?}`;
  `STATE_SCHEMA_VERSION = 17`; `Controller.think(view, tick)` sees no events.
- Objectives count presence by x, y within the radius: men in a house inside the ring hold it.
- The only golden with AI commanders is `platoon-vehicles-s1`; every behaviour.json change regenerates all seven
  (catalog fingerprint).
- Defence siting costs ~0.4 s on the defender's first think (DECISIONS "Defence siting"); timing tests have no AI.

## 2. Invariants
1. The AI acts only through orders (new optional order fields only); the soldiers' own rules live in the sim
   (decisions/drills/occupy), apply to every side and are switchable in data (0 = off).
2. Determinism: named streams only (none new expected), id-ordered loops, ties broken by index; maths via
   `sim/math.ts`; every soldier height via `standZ`; every route via `routeBetween`.
3. New state is optional plain data: `SoldierState.houseDanger?`, `Unit.leftHouse?`, `Drill` clear fields,
   commander `Memory` fields; each gets a save/load hash test. Schema version bumped only if a required shape
   changes (not expected).
4. Values: soldier/drill values in `behaviour.json` (`soldier.house*`, `drills.clear*`, with a note);
   commander/siting values in `COMMANDER` / `SITING` (the established AI exception).
5. Events carry keys (`radio.houseLeft`, `radio.houseCleared`, `radio.clearing`), text in string tables (de, ru, xf).
6. `--no-interiors` maps: no window candidates, no house rules fire: batches with `--no-interiors` stay as now.
7. Perf: bench-vehicles >= 8x (no new per-tick work for men outdoors); defender first think <= +300 ms over today.

## 3. Software decisions (made here, logged in DECISIONS.md with the step that builds them)
- **D1 Occupy one window.** `occupy` gets optional `window?: number` (index into `windowPlaces(ctx, bi)`, a pure
  function of the map): a man (or a one-man order) goes to exactly that place. The AI places men window by window;
  the player's Occupy is unchanged.
- **D2 House choice in siting.** Houses are candidates inside `siteDefence` (not a separate pass) so one score
  trades a window against an outdoor spot; the mix (inside cap per squad, men outside, room cap) is enforced there.
- **D3 Leaving is soldier behaviour in the sim**, like the beaten-zone rule: it applies to player squads too (a
  player's Occupy is not a promise to die in a burning house). It is decided per man, and spreads to his unit's men in
  the same house. Data switch `soldier.houseLeave` (owner question listed).
- **D4 Danger knowledge without events.** `detonate` (explosions.ts) marks `houseDanger {bi, n, tick}` on able men
  inside (or within `houseDangerNearM` of the walls of) the building where a grenade or HE round bursts in/on it. The
  sim never reads its own event stream.
- **D5 Clearing is a drill.** `assault` gets optional `building?: number`; the drill kind `"clear"` reuses the
  assault's groups (halves) and covering-fire code; its own optional fields (`building`, `rooms`, `room`, `stage`,
  `stack`, `entry`) are plain data. The player gets no Clear button in this milestone (owner question).
- **D6 Room order.** Breadth-first over the interior graph from the entry door (ground floor first, then up the
  stairs), rooms with a known enemy first among equals, ties by room id.
- **D7 Grenade in.** Thrower at a stack point beside the opening (1-2 m off the wall, not in front of it), aim point
  the room's centre behind it; existing grenade action and `throwArcExists`. No arc: the next opening; none: the
  room is entered under assault fire.
- **D8 Commander picks houses** on its axis (`COMMANDER.clear`): houses in the objective ring + 20 m and those within
  `corridorM` of the assault element's way in; a house is cleared if a known enemy was in it in the last 60 s or it
  lies in the ring; others are passed. Order by distance along the way in.
- **D9 Hold what is taken.** The clearing squad occupies the house with the defence's mixed spread (D2 rules,
  `window` orders), facing the next objective; the commander keeps `held` houses in memory and does not pull a
  squad out of a house in the main ring for the second objective unless it is the only fit squad.
- **D10 Measuring.** `npm run batch` gets a "buildings" block (tools only); a new lab `npm run lab -- house`
  (modes `defend`, `leave`, `clear`); a new scenario `house-clear` with golden `house-clear-s1` (polish).

## 4. Steps

### Step 1 — defend
**Build.**
- `sim/systems/occupy.ts` + `orders.ts`: `occupy.window?` (D1); validation (index in range, building with
  interior); the man's `homePosture`/end heading from the window place.
- `ai/src/siting.ts`: window candidates for every house whose footprint lies within `searchM` of the group's place
  (and inside its ring): each window place scored like an outdoor spot but seen from its own height
  (`standZ` + eye height of its posture), cover from the walls (`windowCoverW`), with
  - **mix:** at most `insideShare` (0.6, rounded down) of a group inside, at least `outsideMin` 2 men outside when any
    is inside; outside men at least `wallClearM` 8 m from the walls of an occupied house (one shell on the house);
  - **room cap** `roomMax` 2 per room (any floor), `houseMax` 4 per house;
  - **floors:** an upper window only if it sees `upperGain` 1.2x the best ground window of that wall, at most half
    the men inside upstairs;
  - **the back:** one outside man per occupied house on its far side from the front, facing the flanks/rear
    (`backWatch`);
  - **facing:** window men the window's heading; outside men the middle of what they see (as now).
- `ai/src/commander.ts`: window places ordered as `occupy {building, window}` at a walk; the rest as now.
- `tools`: batch "buildings" block (indoors, upper, wounds in/out, grenades at houses, HE on houses, cluster at
  240 s) and `lab -- house defend` (defending squad sited around one house, scripted attacker fire and HE).
**Acceptance (baseline -> target).**
- Defender man-seconds indoors, village: 0.9 % (platoon), 1.7 % (mounted) -> **25-60 %**; farmland 0.4 % -> reported
  (fewer houses: 4 near the rings vs 12).
- Defender upper-floor man-seconds, village: 0 % -> **>= 5 %**.
- Every squad with men inside has **>= 2 men outside** at 240 s (100 %); **no room > 2 men, no house > 4** (100 %).
- Clustering at 239 s: max within 10 m **<= 5** on every seed, mean not above baseline (3.1 / 3.5 / 2.2).
- Whole-defence share of the approach seen, village: 0.40 -> **>= 0.50**.
- Defender wounds per 100 man-minutes inside vs outside: reported (the point is fewer for men inside).
- Balance reported (platoon, platoon-ru, platoon-vehicles; not re-tuned). Defender first think <= +300 ms.
**Batches.** platoon and platoon-ru 20 seeds x 1200 s; platoon-vehicles 15 seeds; village-occupy 20 x 300 s
(unchanged behaviour expected, golden changes only by fingerprint if behaviour.json changed).
**Tests.** `sim/test/occupy.test.ts` (or floors): `window` order puts one man at that place, sill posture, bad
index rejected, save/load hash mid-route. `tools/test/siting.test.ts`: village seed: some window places chosen,
caps (room 2, house 4, inside share, >= 2 outside, wall clearance), upper rule, back watcher, deterministic,
interiors off = no window places (same result as today on a no-interiors map).

### Step 2 — leave
**Build** (`sim/systems/decisions.ts`, `explosions.ts`; data `soldier.house*`).
- `houseDanger` (D4). **Trap:** `houseTrapBursts` 2 bursts in/on his house within `houseTrapSeconds` 30 s (one HE
  round on the roof counts 2).
- **Enemy inside:** an enemy he or his unit knows (seen or heard, last `houseEnemySeconds` 5 s) inside his building.
  Exception: an enemy in his own room within 4 m is fought (assault fire), not fled from.
- **Outflanked:** an enemy known within `houseFlankM` 150 m whose bearing from the house is more than
  `houseFlankDeg` 120 deg off the unit's front in that house (mean window facing of its men there).
- **Leaving:** every man of his unit in that house goes, at a run, out by the exit away from the threat, to a covered
  outdoor spot (`bestCoverNear`, exposure < 0.9) `houseLeaveM` 25-50 m away, on the side away from the threat
  (never toward it), keeping `minSpacingM`; `Unit.leftHouse {building, tick, reason}` keeps the unit (AI and its own
  rules) out of it for `houseReenterSeconds` 120 s. Radio `radio.houseLeft` with the reason key.
- `ai/src/commander.ts`: a squad that left re-sites around where it went (outdoor siting, 20 m), as after a rally.
**Acceptance** (lab `house leave`, 20 trials per trigger; batches as step 1).
- Trap: men out within 20 s after the 2nd burst: **0 % today -> >= 80 %**; defender casualties in the lab's trap
  trial (2 grenades + 4 HE on the house) **at least 30 % below** the no-leave run (same seeds, rule off).
- Enemy inside: out within 15 s (or fighting in his room) **>= 80 %**. Outflanked: out within 20 s **>= 80 %**.
- Destination in cover (exposure < 0.9) and within 60 deg of straight away from the threat **>= 80 %**.
- No false leaving: village-occupy 20 x 300 s keeps **>= 85 %** of defender man-seconds indoors (89 % today) and
  no `houseLeft` without a trigger; step-1 indoors share in the village stays >= 20 %.
- Batches: defender wounds per 100 man-min inside not worse than step 1; balance reported.
**Tests.** `sim/test/house-leave.test.ts`: each trigger leaves, no trigger stays, enemy in his room is fought,
spreads to the unit's men in that house only, no re-entry for 120 s, player occupy covered, data switch off = today,
save/load hash with `houseDanger` and `leftHouse` mid-leave. Architecture test still green (`routeBetween`,
`standZ`).

### Step 3 — clear
**Build** (sim drill + commander; data `drills.clear*`, `COMMANDER.clear`).
- `assault {x, y, building?}` (D5). Drill stages: **suppress** (the covering half fires at the window places facing
  it, `FireTask.suppress`, assault fire rules; the runners bound up as now); **close** (runners to the stack point
  beside the chosen opening: a door, else a ground-floor window, the one least seen from the house's other
  windows); **grenade** (D7: one or two men throw into the room behind it; the stack crouches against the wall and
  waits for the bursts, `clearAfterBurstS` 1 s); **enter** (two to three men into the room by the opening/door,
  `indoorSpots` of that room, assault fire on the move; the rest follow to the doorway); **next room** (grenade from
  the doorway into the next room by D6, enter; stairs: grenade up the stairwell, then up); **done** when every room
  was entered and no enemy is known inside, or `clearTimeoutS`; then **hold** (D9). The drill ends early when the
  squad is below `clearMinMen` 3 able men (it holds what it has, else withdraws as an assault would).
- `ai/src/commander.ts` (D8): during the assault (and second objective), each assaulting squad gets the houses on its
  axis in order: `assault {building}` per house, then the objective; a squad holding a taken house keeps it.
- lab `house clear`: one attacking squad (scripted: suppress, then clear order) vs the step-1/2 defence of one house
  and vs the old occupy-all defence.
**Acceptance.**
- Clearing (clear.mts setup, 12+ seeds, same squads): house taken **2/12 -> >= 5/12**; attackers down per battle
  **4.9 -> <= 4.0**; exchange (attacker / defender down) **3.9 -> <= 2.5**; attacker grenades into the house
  **0 -> >= 2 per attempt**; median first man in at or before 300 s.
- Lab: a grenade thrown through a window or door lands in the room behind it in >= 70 % of throws.
- platoon (village) batches: attacker man-seconds indoors **0.8 % -> >= 5 %**; grenades aimed at houses
  **1.1 -> >= 4** per battle; in battles the attacker wins, at least one house in the main ring is held by it at the
  end in **>= 80 %**. Balance reported (no tuning for balance alone; flag if village attacker wins fall below 1/20).
**Tests.** `sim/test/clear.test.ts`: room order (BFS, enemy rooms first, deterministic); stack point beside, not
in front of, the opening; a grenade through a ground-floor window lands inside; nobody enters before the burst; a
room is entered after it; upper floor via stairs; drill ends with all rooms visited and occupies; fails safe (no
arc, men down); save/load hash mid-clear; fixture faction `xf` clears. `tools/test/commander.test.ts`: the attacker
picks the houses on its axis (and passes those off it), holds a taken house, memory plain data, deterministic.

## 5. Risks
- Window places see little in some houses (walls facing away): the score already handles it; the inside share is a
  cap, not a quota.
- Upper floors are traps (stairs slow the way out): step 2's leave rule starts upstairs men first; measured.
- Grenade arcs through windows may be rare from a stack point: D7 falls back to the next opening, then assault fire;
  the lab measures the rate before the commander relies on it.
- Defender siting cost grows with window candidates: candidates limited to houses within `searchM`; measured.
- Balance in the village may swing either way; reported, not tuned (owner).
