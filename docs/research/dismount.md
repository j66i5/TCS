**IFV support by fire (RU):** the BMP supports the dismounted men with its own fire
(https://bigenc.ru/c/boevaia-mashina-pekhoty-e4bdbf: "Действия спешенных мотострелков БМП поддерживают своим огнём",
medium). A Ukrainian teaching text on Soviet-style squad drill: after dismounting the squad forms a chain and the BMPs
"move behind the chain, on its flank, sometimes in the gaps, using folds of the ground" (search extract cut off before
the distance; https://vukladach.pp.ua/MyWeb/manual/pidruchnuku13122023/Defense%20of%20Ukraine/Presentation/6.3.4.pdf,
low). Cold-war US reading: infantry within 200 m of the tanks, IFVs 100–400 m behind the tanks
(https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/policy/usmc/mcwp/3-15-5/cdraft_ch2.pdf, low). Current Russian
practice adds BMP-2M indirect fire from closed positions (https://iz.ru/2133838/2026-07-17/ekipazhi-bmp-2m-otrabatyvaiut-strelbu-s-zakrytykh-pozitcii,
medium) — not relevant at our scale.

**Recommended game value (RU / BMP-2): dismount 400 m from the objective**, at the nearest point at or beyond 400 m
that is out of sight of known enemies (dead ground / behind cover); if none exists, still dismount at 400 m (doctrine
says "covered if possible"). The BMPs then **follow the dismounted chain about 100–200 m behind it, on the flanks, from
cover to cover, firing on the move/short halts** — i.e. they close to ~200–300 m of the objective while supporting.
Justification: the 2005 combat manual wording plus the explicit "up to 400 m" in study notes; 400 m also matches the
safe distance from own artillery for an attack on foot.

**Recommended game value (DE / Puma): dismount 300 m from the objective, at a point out of sight of known enemies**
(the last covered point — wood edge, crest, buildings — at or beyond 300 m; if the covered point is closer, e.g.
200 m, use it, since doctrine says "as late as possible"). The Pumas then **stay in a covered/hull-down
overwatch position at about 400–600 m from the objective (at or just behind the dismount point) and support by fire
from there**, lifting fire as the grenadiers close; they do not follow the dismounts forward. Justification:
Bundeswehr principle "mounted as long as possible" and "dismount where the enemy cannot observe" argues for later
dismount than Russian doctrine; 300 m is a game choice inside the 500 m grenadier–tank spacing, not a sourced number
(confidence low).

## Summary for the AI

| Side | Dismount distance from objective | Condition | IFV after dismount |
|---|---|---|---|
| RU (BMP-2) | 400 m | prefer a point out of sight of known enemies; dismount anyway at 400 m if none | follow the chain 100–200 m behind, on the flanks, cover to cover, firing |
| DE (Puma) | 300 m (closer if the last covered point is closer) | must be out of sight of known enemies; keep moving mounted until such a point | overwatch from covered position ~400–600 m from the objective, support by fire, do not follow |
