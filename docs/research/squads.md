## 1. German Panzergrenadiergruppe on Schützenpanzer Puma (3 crew + 6 dismounts)

| Position | Role | Typical rank | Weapon(s) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crew 1 | Kommandant = Gruppenführer | not confirmed | not confirmed | https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puma_(Sch%C3%BCtzenpanzer) |
| Crew 2 | Richtschütze ("Truppführer Waffensystem") | not confirmed | turret: MK30-2, MG4A1 coax | https://www.bundeswehr.de/de/ausruestung-technik-bundeswehr/landsysteme-bundeswehr/schuetzenpanzer-puma |
| Crew 3 | Fahrer | not confirmed | not confirmed | same |
| Dismount 1 | Truppführer, leads the Schützentrupp on foot | one example: Oberstabsgefreiter | G36 (assumed) | https://www.bundeswehr.de/de/meldungen/schuetzenpanzer-puma-im-angriff-5579452 |
| Dismount 2 | MG-Schütze | not confirmed | MG5 (infantry version); MG4 also in the mix | https://www.bundeswehr.de/de/organisation/heer/aktuelles/gefechtsschiessen-angehender-panzergrenadiere-6159218 , https://esut.de/2020/08/fachbeitraege/20769/umsetzung-der-faehigkeitsforderungen-des-schuetzenpanzers-puma-in-das-system-panzergrenadier/ |
| Dismount 3 | Panzerfaustschütze | not confirmed | Panzerfaust 3 + G36 | de.wikipedia Puma (above) |
| Dismount 4 | Gruppenzielfernrohrschütze | not confirmed | G28 or G27P | role: de.wikipedia; rifles: https://esut.de/2023/06/fachbeitraege/ruestung/42230/die-handfeuerwaffenprojekte-der-bundeswehr-ein-komprimierter-sachstand/ |
| Dismount 5–6 | Panzergrenadiere | not confirmed | G36; Granatpistole or second Panzerfaust by task | de.wikipedia |

- **Who dismounts:** commander, gunner and driver stay in the vehicle and support; the Truppführer leads on
  foot (bundeswehr.de "puma-im-angriff", above). Other sources say it depends on the situation.
- **Alternative dismount mix:** close-security soldier with under-barrel launcher, MG gunner, optional second
  MG gunner, two Panzerfaust gunners (https://suv.report/die-zukunft-des-spz-puma-2-los-konfigurationsstand-s1/).
- **Tank-hunting team:** three soldiers with two Panzerfäuste and the Granatpistole (bundeswehr.de article).
- **Load (instructor quote):** G36 with five magazines, five hand grenades, "a few" smoke pots; by task the
  Granatpistole, MG5 with 500 rounds, or Panzerfaust
  (https://www.bundeswehr.de/de/meldungen/panzergrenadiere-spezialgrundausbildung-5572818).
- **Vest:** MOBAST, hard plates SK4 (up to 7.62x51), soft armour SK1
  (https://esut.de/2024/12/meldungen/55553/das-200-000ste-schutzwesten-system-mobast-ist-ausgeliefert/). Mass not found.
- **Helmet:** "Gefechtshelm Streitkräfte" replacing the aramid M92
  (https://www.pcwelt.de/article/1433969/gefechtshelm-streitkrafte-das-ist-der-neue-helm-der-bundeswehr.html). Mass not found.
- **IdZ-ES:** part of "System Panzergrenadier"
  (https://www.rheinmetall.com/en/media/news-watch/news/2021/2021-03-19_german-army-declares-system-panzergrenadier-fit-to-fight);
  helmet requirement 1.4 kg (https://www.soldiermod.com/volume-8/idz-es.html).
- **G95:** first rifles handed over December 2025 (https://www.bundeswehr.de/de/meldungen/g95-neuer-standard);
  the G36 is still the majority weapon.

## 2. Russian motor rifle squad on BMP-2 / BMP-3

| Position | Role | Typical rank | Weapon(s) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Squad leader = vehicle commander; dismounts, leads the fire group | serzhant | AK-74M | https://www.benning.army.mil/infantry/magazine/issues/2023/Winter/pdf/8_Grau-BartlesBreach_txt.pdf |
| 2 | Gunner-operator, deputy vehicle commander; stays mounted | ryadovoy or yefreytor (sources differ) | 30 mm 2A42 (BMP-2) | https://www.battleorder.org/rus-bmp-platoon |
| 3 | Driver-mechanic; stays mounted | ryadovoy | AKS-74U | https://studfile.net/preview/9122760/page:17/ |
| 4 | Machine gunner (fire group) | ryadovoy | PKP or RPK-74 | battleorder (above) |
| 5 | Grenadier (fire group) | ryadovoy | RPG-7V/V2 + AK-74M, 2 rockets | battleorder |
| 6 | Assistant grenadier (fire group) | ryadovoy | AK-74M + 3 rockets | battleorder |
| 7 | Senior rifleman, leads the manoeuvre group | yefreytor | AK-74M + GP-25/GP-30 | https://kriegsimulation.blogspot.com/2014/12/russian-ground-forces-squad-tactics-fix.html |
| 8 (9) | Rifleman (one or two) | ryadovoy | AK-74M + GP-25 | https://base.garant.ru/72245250/ |

- **Squad size:** 8 by Russian training material
  (https://infourok.ru/organizacionno-shtatnaya-struktura-motostrelkovogo-otdeleniya-vzvoda-obzr-8-klass-7346828.html);
  9 (two crew + seven dismounts incl. leader) by Grau/Bartles (Benning link); 6–7 dismounts + 2 crew by a US
  TRADOC video (https://www.dvidshub.net/video/1005571/russian-rifle-squad-assault-threatminutes).
- **SVD:** one sniper per squad in BTR/MT-LB squads (battleorder); older FM 100-2-3: one SVD per platoon
  (https://irp.fas.org/doddir/army/fm100-2-3.pdf).
- **Wartime practice:** full-strength squads are rare, often five dismounts; fire group kept manned (Benning
  link); infantry stratified into line, assault, specialised and disposable troops
  (https://rusi.org/explore-our-research/publications/special-resources/meatgrinder-russian-tactics-second-year-its-invasion-ukraine).
- **Rifle ammunition:** unit of fire 450 rounds, 180 on the soldier
  (https://vuts-miit.ru/library/%D0%9E%D0%B1%D1%89%D0%B0%D1%8F_%D1%82%D0%B0%D0%BA%D1%82%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%B0.pdf).
- **Machine gun:** 600 rounds PKP/PKM (forum only:
  https://community.battlefront.com/topic/121355-modern-russian-infantry-battalion-structure/).
- **6B45 vest:** about 7.5–8 kg; class given as BR5, BR6 or GOST 6 depending on source
  (https://armyrecognition.com/focus-analysis-conflicts/army/defence-security-industry-technology/russian-soldiers-will-receive-new-6b43-6b45-body-armour-and-6b47-combat-helmets-tass-12705162 ,
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratnik_(program)).
- **6B47 helmet:** 1.0–1.25 kg, pistol/fragment level
  (https://www.thefirearmblog.com/blog/2015/10/07/the-ratnik-russias-warrior-of-the-future).
- **AK-12:** 2025 state contract reported fulfilled
  (https://www1.ru/news/2025/11/24/kalasnikov-vypolnil-godovoi-kontrakt-na-postavku-avtomatov-ak-12-zakazciku.html);
  share versus AK-74M in motor rifle units not found.
